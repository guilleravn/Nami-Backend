# Decisions

Why the stack and the process are the way they are. Add an entry when you make a decision a newcomer would otherwise question. Never rewrite an old entry: supersede it with a new one.

Format:

```md
## D-n: <decision>

**Context:** the problem and the constraints.

**Decision:** what we chose.

**Consequences:** what it makes easier, what it makes harder, and what we gave up.
```
